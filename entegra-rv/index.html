MSRP is the manufacturer’s suggested retail price, not necessarily a price previously charged. MSRP for on-order or coming-soon RVs may be estimated from manufacturer information and can change on arrival. Displayed savings percentages are rounded. “Regular advertised price” is supplied by the current inventory feed and does not establish how long that price was offered. Sale prices include applicable advertised incentives, offers and rebates unless specified otherwise in writing. Offers cannot be assumed to combine. Prices and payment estimates exclude tax, title, registration, licensing, insurance and applicable charges unless itemized otherwise. Request a written total. Default payment illustrations use 9.99% APR, 20% down and 240 monthly payments; the assumptions shown beside a calculator or inventory search control its displayed estimates. These examples are not credit offers or advertised available rates. Used RVs and lower financed amounts may qualify for different rates or shorter terms. Actual terms depend on lender approval and the vehicle. Financing inquiries on this site are requests for a conversation, not credit applications or approvals.

Deposits: Under MHSRV’s published purchase terms, deposits are non-refundable unless otherwise specified in writing.
