Full pricing, financing & promotional terms

Information is believed reliable but is not guaranteed. Confirm the exact stock number, selling price, availability and installed equipment with the dealership before purchase. Inventory is an imported snapshot. Photos and videos may show representative vehicles, model years or optional equipment. Manufacturer features, dimensions, weights and capacities can change and must be verified on the actual unit.

MSRP is the manufacturer’s suggested retail price, not necessarily a price previously charged. MSRP for on-order or coming-soon RVs may be estimated from manufacturer information and can change on arrival. Displayed savings percentages are rounded. “Regular advertised price” is supplied by the current inventory feed and does not establish how long that price was offered. Deals include feed-marked specials and units priced at least 42% below listed MSRP; they do not establish a limited-time deadline.

Sale prices include applicable advertised incentives, offers and rebates unless specified otherwise in writing. Offers cannot be assumed to combine. Prices and promotions are subject to change, including after a promotion ends. Any advertised minimum discount applies only to qualifying vehicles and is not a promise for every RV. Contact sales for the offer terms and eligibility for your stock number.

Prices and payment estimates exclude tax, title, registration, licensing, insurance and applicable charges unless itemized otherwise. Request a written total. Default payment illustrations use 9.99% APR, 20% down and 240 monthly payments; the assumptions shown beside a calculator or inventory search control its displayed estimates. These examples are not credit offers or advertised available rates. Used RVs and lower financed amounts may qualify for different rates or shorter terms. Actual terms depend on lender approval and the vehicle. Financing inquiries on this site are requests for a conversation, not credit applications or approvals.

Deposits: Under MHSRV’s published purchase terms, deposits are non-refundable unless otherwise specified in writing. A deposit requests that the dealership hold and prepare the selected unit for delivery and orientation. Failure to pay by the agreed delivery date may forfeit the deposit; MHSRV retains discretion to apply part or all of it toward a future purchase. Review and agree to the written deposit and purchase terms with the dealership before paying.
