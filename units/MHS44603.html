Read the full dealer description

The 2027 Foretravel Realm FL605 is, not only, the premium luxury Motor-Coach on the market today, but the only coach in the industry built on a Freightliner TravelRide IV-SL chassis offering incomparable ride, handling, and safety. This extraordinary motor coach is boasting the extraordinary Apex interior décor. The interior of the coach is paired perfectly with the Ares exterior paint scheme. You will also find a multi-function digital dash and instrumentation display system, the Premier Steer adjustable driver's assist system, a Navigation system with in-dash and additional passenger side monitor, Total Coach Monitoring System, tire pressure sensors, beautiful tile floors and back-splashes, quartz counter tops throughout, ultra-high-end appliances, LED accent lighting throughout, a beautiful curved step entry way, Braun extra heavy duty power entrance step, a designer entry door with LED accent lighting, iPad launch system, 4K TVs where applicable, upgraded cab stereo and sub-woofer, heated and cooled pilot and co-pilot seats, recessed and upgraded ceiling features in the galley, recessed cook top, Mobile Eye Collision Avoidance System, a 'Bird's Eye View' camera system for the ultimate in coach visibility along with an additional 3-camera coach monitoring system with power rear camera, dual integrated power awnings, power entry door awning, exterior entertainment center, (2) electric sliding cargo trays, exterior freezer, chrome grill and accent package, (1) 3000W inverter, electric floor heat and (2) solar panels. You will know instantly, once behind the wheel of a Realm FS6, that this chassis is truly a cut above all other luxury motor coach chassis. It is powered by a Cummins X15 605HP diesel. You will also enjoy the ultimate in slide-out room fit and finish. These slides are undoubtedly head and shoulders above the competition. They feature pneumatic seals that provide a literal airtight seal completely around the entire slide-out room regardless of slide position for the premium in fit, finish, and function. For more details contact Motor Home Specialist today. - Realm, by definition, is a royal kingdom; a domain within which anything may occur, prevail or dominate. The Realm of Dreams is here and available exclusively at Motor Home Specialist, the #1 Volume Selling Motor Home Dealership in the World. Visit MHSRV.com or call 800-335-6054 for complete details, photos, videos, brochures, and more. The Foretravel Realm FS6... Your Kingdom Awaits.
