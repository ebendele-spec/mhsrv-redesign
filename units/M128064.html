More About This Super C This layout is built for people who actually live in their coach, not just pass through it. At the back, the rear bathroom feels like a private suite, with washer and dryer prep tucked in so longer trips stay practical, not chaotic. Moving forward, the bedroom centers around a true residential-style king bed, with room to walk around and settle in instead of squeezing past the bedding. Just outside the bedroom, a thoughtful drop zone with coat hooks and shoe storage makes it easy to keep the main walkway clear when you’re coming and going. In the living area, the booth dinette gives you a real 'sit down and stay awhile' space for meals or laptop time, and up front the swivel cab seats turn the cockpit into part of the lounge, so everyone can face in and relax once you’re parked. The Newmar Supreme Aire Super C diesel motorhomes continue to raise the bar with even more luxury and innovation you will not find on most other Super C coaches. Built on the Freightliner Cascadia chassis with serious diesel power and heavy-duty towing capability, they are made for confident travel whether your destination is the coast, the mountains, or anywhere in between. Inside, the Supreme Aire feels more like a high-end residence than a motorhome, with refined cabinetry, premium seating options, and a thoughtfully designed living area that can be tailored to how you relax. A sky window above the cockpit brings in natural light when you want it, and the power shade lets you dial it back for movie nights or mid-day naps. Up front, air-ride captain’s chairs, a modern digital dash, and the Xite infotainment system work together to make every mile feel smooth, quiet, and under control.
