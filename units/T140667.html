About this RV

MSRP $128,015.

New Brinkley 5th Wheel for sale - 2026 Brinkley Model Z 3200 is approximately 34' 11' in length with 3 slides.

Features Dual Pane Windows Curt TCS Upgraded Suspension Integrity Package Power Package Insulation Package Fireplace More About This RV Escape to a world of comfort and luxury with this incredible fifth wheel! The living area features a plush L-sofa, five recliners, and a warm fireplace.

The versatile dining table/L desk seats up to four people, making it perfect for meals, work, or play.

Ample shoe storage and a convenient spot for pet bowls enhance comfort and practicality.

The kitchen offers stunning solid surface countertops, dishwasher prep, a residential microwave, and a three-burner cooktop.

The expansive pantry with pull-out drawers provides ample storage for your favorite ingredients and cooking essentials.

The full bathroom is a serene oasis, featuring a linen closet for added storage and convenience.

The front bedroom is a peaceful sanctuary, featuring a spacious king bed, shelves, and a sliding dresser top with hidden storage.

The generous wardrobe and shoe rack provide ample storage for your clothes and shoes.

The Brinkley mid-profile Model Z fifth wheels bring you clean lines and an upscale look with plenty of style and exciting new features like the industry first brass plumbing connections.

These units also feature an automotive grade front cap, plenty of exterior storage, exterior hot and cold showers, and wide stance leveling jacks for easy set up.

Residential frameless windows provide a clean look and beautiful views, and ample electrical outlets and 6 USB/USB-C outlets allow all of your electronics and appliances to easily be used and charged.
