About this RV

MSRP $162,105. New 2027 Entegra Ethos 20T. Optional Features Customer Value Package E-Z Drive™: Hellwig helper springs, premium heavy-duty suspension Truma Combi G Comfort Plus Water heater and Furnace Electric Patio Awning with LED light Strip 6-way adjustment driver and passenger seats 2,000W inverter 200W Solar Shop hundreds of motorhomes including Class B RVs at MHSRV! Choose from Midwest Automotive Designs Patriot – Patriot Cruiser – Forest River OGV V RV – V Cruise – Thor Motor Coach Tranquility – Entegra Coach Launch and more. Visit MHSRV.com for our complete line-up and the sale prices. About This RV Ethos is powered by the RAM® ProMaster 3500 chassis, brought to life by a 3.6L V6 24V VVT 276HP engine and is approximately 20 feet, 11 inches in length. Inside, custom-molded ABS interior wall panels, a Uconnect® 5 NAV infotainment center and a swivel passenger an driver seats bring high-end luxury to van life. This RV features a 2-Year, 24,000-mile Warranty, 100% top-down Pre-Delivery Inspection, improved ride and handling from being upfitted with Helwig Helper Springs. About Us For more details on this unit, our full inventory, brochures, window stickers, videos, photos, and customer reviews, visit MHSRV.com or call 800-335-6054 for the Texas location or 334-288-0331 for our Montgomery location. At Motor Home Specialist, we never charge prep or orientation fees. Every new RV sale includes a multi-point inspection, full interior and exterior detail, and a high-pressure rain booth test—an exclusive service in the industry.
