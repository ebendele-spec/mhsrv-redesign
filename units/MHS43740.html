About this RV

M.S.R.P. $265,155 - The New 2026 Entegra Launch 19Y AWD is brought to life by a 2.0L 4-Cylinder diesel engine with 211HP and 332 ft.-lb. torque and is approximately 19 feet 7 inches in length.

Read the full dealer description

This impressive vehicle includes the optional customer value package. The new 2026 launch features the lithionics system which includes a 48V lithium battery bringing 165 Ah, 3,000W Victron Pure Sine Wave inverter, NeverDie battery management system, 48V secondary alternator, Intermotive High-Idle charge system, 12,500 48V Coleman Mach 10 air conditioner and 200W solar. The extremely capable Launch AWD Class B RV features luxurious amenities like Technoform® European-style cabinetry provide a sleek, modern look while a Firefly Multiplex system provides ease of use. The Launch was made for adventure with the armless patio awning with LED light strip and wind sensor, double roof rack, starlink satellite internet system and Hydronic heating system with LED touchscreen control. This amazing new motor home also includes a moveable roof ladder, stainless-steel sink, lane-keeping assist & blind spot assist, keyless entry system, rear garage under the bed, MaxxAir Deluxe roof vent, heated grey tank (12V pad) with skid plate and interior-mounted freshwater tanks and so much more.
