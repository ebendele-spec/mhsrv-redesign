About This RV This RV measures approximately 35 feet 11 inches in length and features 2 slide outs, large living area, power loft bed, pull-out countertop, flip-up countertop and a king size bed. The FR3 crossover motorhome combines the family friendly livability and affordability of an easy to drive Class C motorhome with the space, convenience, and styling of a Class A motorhome. This beautiful new motorhome also features the new Ford chassis with 7.3L PFI V-8, a 6-speed TorqShift® automatic transmission. A few of the standard features the beautiful FR3 boasts include slide room topper awnings, power patio awning with LED light strip, 4-point hydraulic leveling jacks, LED lighting throughout unit, exterior shower, power front sun shade, 6KW generator, 3 burner cook top with oven and flush mount stove cover and so much more.
