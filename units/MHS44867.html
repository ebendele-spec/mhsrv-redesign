About This RV When you want Super C muscle without giving up true luxury-coach finish, the 2027 Entegra Accolade XL 33J answers the call. It rides on the proven Freightliner S2RV Plus chassis paired with the E-Z Drive Premier ride-and-handling package, SACHS shocks, a computer-balanced driveshaft, rear air suspension, full air brakes, and a heavy-duty stabilizer bar, for a quiet, planted, confident ride mile after mile. Under the hood, a Cummins turbo-diesel with a exhaust brake delivers the strength to pull grades and tow with authority, while backup and side-view cameras and a Sony infotainment center with Apple CarPlay and Android Auto keep the cockpit modern and command easy. Step inside and the Super C toughness gives way to genuine residential craftsmanship: hand-laid porcelain tile, solid hardwood cabinetry and stiles with deluxe hidden hinges, solid surface countertops, and premium fabrics throughout.
