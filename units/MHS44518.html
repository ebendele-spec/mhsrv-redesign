About this RV

MSRP $129,346. New 2027 Thor Motor Coach Four Winds® 19Z. Features Hampton Collection More About This RV This RV measures approximately 20 feet 5 inches in length providing a sleeping, dining and living space while still remaining compact enough for most any state park and exceptional maneuverability even for first time RVers! This features the Ford E-Series Chassis and the Ford 7.3L V8. 325HP engine with 450 lb.-ft Torque. Additional features and construction highlights include an One-piece fiberglass front cap, one-piece upper rear cap, premium one-piece crossflex exterior roofing, power patio awning with integrated LED lighting, Drop down stairwell entrance step with accent light under step, exterior grab handle, 7” Touchscreen dash radio with Bluetooth, Android Auto, Apple CarPlay, keyless entry key fob, back-up monitor, full extension soft close metal ball-bearing drawer guides, cab-over sleeping area with cup holders and bunk ladder, flip-up countertop extension, 4.0KW gas generator, automatic transfer switch with power protection and tankless water heater, front overhead bunk vent with shade, AGS15 wall mounted LCD system panel screen, manual e-start switch in dash and much more.
