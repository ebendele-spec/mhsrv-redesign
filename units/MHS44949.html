About This RV This RV is a magnificent example of luxury and power in the realm of Class C diesel motorhomes. This model is designed to elevate your travel experience with its exceptional features such as having 2 slides and a king sized bed and stunning design. Built on a robust Freightliner S2RV Plus chassis, the Europa houses a formidable Cummins 6.7L Turbo Diesel engine, delivering 360 horsepower and an impressive 800 ft.-lbs. of torque. The dual air conditioning units provide comfort in any climate, making this RV perfect for any adventure, wherever the road may take you. Additional features include the SACHS Front & Rear Shock Absorbers, rear sway bar, 32' LED Smart TV in the bedroom and USB Charging Ports.
