Features Maxx Air Fan in Bathroom Folding Dinette Chairs Ultimate Package Power Package Insulation Package More About This RV This is the perfect travel trailer for the couple looking for comfort at the campground! Once you've unloaded the pass-thru storage, you can put out the 17' patio awning and relax in your camp chairs. Head inside to make lunch in the fully equipped kitchen that includes a three-burner range, 30' convection microwave, and dishwasher prep. There is a pantry with outlets within the large slide out, along with a 10-cu. ft. refrigerator that you can access with the slide in, plus theater seating across from the TV. And if you need sleeping space for guests, just choose the optional tri-fold sofa in place of the theater seating! A pull-out table with a drawer provides a place to dine together, and the entryway closet will be perfect to keep jackets and shoes tidy. You'll get a great night's rest on the front queen bed with four drawers, nightstand cubbies, and a room divider for a little more privacy. The Model Ix by Brinkley is your ticket to lightweight towing and exceptional features. This premium half-ton towable travel trailer features Brinkley's CraftSense building materials for a higher level of construction, along with a seamless laminated aluminum exterior with automotive grade full-body paint and modern graphics. You will stay powered up thanks to the power package with a 200-watt solar panel, and the Road Armor suspension will help provide a smooth tow from home to campground. The interior of the Model Ix includes premium residential cabinets with dovetail drawers, an ultra quiet and efficient A/C system, and a SoundCore Boom 2 Bluetooth waterproof speaker so you can listen to your favorite tunes indoors or outdoors!
