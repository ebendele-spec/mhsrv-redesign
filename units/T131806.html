About this RV

MSRP $100,668. 2026 Brinkley Model Z Air 297 is approximately 34' 11' in length with 2 slides. Features Theater Seating Integrity Package Power Package Insulation Package Sliding Desk Dinette Table 18K Variable Speed Chill Cube 2nd A/C More About This RV This travel trailer is spacious thanks to the dual opposing slides in the combined kitchen and living area. There is a rear 72' tri-fold sofa here, theater seating, and a sliding desk/dining table. The televator and fireplace will let you relax each evening with a movie or good book, and the full kitchen lets you make meals wherever you go. The chef of your group will also appreciate the pantry with adjustable shelving for all their ingredients, a hutch, 30' residential microwave, and even a pull-out trash bin for easy clean-up. When you're ready to call it a night, the front private bedroom will be waiting for you. This room includes a queen bed with four underbed drawers, a closet with pass-thru access to the bathroom closet, plus washer and dryer prep if you want the option to go full-time! With any Model Z Air travel trailer by Brinkley, you will enjoy premium features at every turn. The industry-first low maintenance roof is sure to be appreciated, plus there are industry-first brass plumbing connections for excellent resistance to corrosion. The drop-frame chassis with ample exterior storage will allow you to bring along camp gear galore, plus there are motion lighting and power outlets for added convenience! You'll also find two hot/cold showers outdoors, along with a 370-watt solar panel, a custom LP box with locked battery storage, and a premium utility center with a retractable 65' water hose to wash off mud from the lake. The interior is just as accommodating with its solid surface countertops, Brinkley CraftSense wood trim construction, integrated window screens and insulated blackout blinds, and the list of luxury features goes on!
