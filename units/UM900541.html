Features Overhead Loft Swivel Cab Seats U-Shaped Dinette More About This RV A couple or family will enjoy the look of the interior each time they take the RV on the road. From the curved cabinets, the lighted soft close galley drawers and the sprung-cushion dinette and sofa seats there will surely be a favorite feature for everyone. The cross-coach storage holds larger items, and the above-bed Anything Keeper storage stows things such as cellphones, keys, chargers, and more. You will be able to easy set up the queen Murphy+ bed when it's time for bedtime while the kids sleep on the cabover bunk or the U-shaped dinette. You might like to add the theater seating option if you don't need that extra sleeping space. Everyone can get cleaned up in the full rear bathroom with a space savings sliding door before making meals with full amenities including a convection microwave/oven. With each View Class C diesel motorhome by Winnebago you can extend your trips off grid with the industry-leading holding tanks, the standard 200W solar, the two deep-cycle, the Group 31 RV batteries, and the 2,000W inverter, plus the 3,500W Cummins Onan Microquiet LP generator. The insulated sleeper deck is built with premium thermal and acoustic insulation providing the SuperShell sleeper deck to be more comfortable and relaxing anywhere during any season. And this motorhome is built on a Mercedes-Benz Sprinter chassis with advanced safety features giving you peace of mind such as the MBUX touchscreen infotainment system with interactive voice interface, navigation, Wi-Fi hotspot and more! The interior offers luxury space and simple solutions for storage, plus vinyl flooring and laminate countertops for easy clean up. Choose a View today and start making memories!
