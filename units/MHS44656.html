About This RV The Georgetown 36F5 measures approximately 39 feet 7 inch in length. Floor plan highlights include (2) slide-out rooms, 2 Full baths, Bunk Beds, a king size bed in the master suite, and a massive living and dining area perfect for the big families and extended stays! It rides the Ford® chassis with high performance 7.3L PFI V-8. You will also find a 6-speed TorqShift® automatic transmission, an updated instrument cluster, automatic headlights and a tilt and telescoping steering wheel. In addition, this RV features the Artic package, forced air heat for holding tanks, CPAP Prep, porcelain toilet, solid surface kitchen countertops and raised panel solid wood cabinet doors.
