About this RV

MSRP $137,047. New 2027 Entegra Arc 18L. Optional Features Ceramic Chassis Exterior Customer Value Package Thule® patio awning with LED light strip Aqua-Hot® GEN-1 hydronic water and heating system Swivel passenger seat with 6-way adjustment 13,500 BTU Coleman®-Mach® 10 air conditioner Hellwig helper spring Variable Speed A/C Shop hundreds of motorhomes including B Van RVs at MHSRV! We also buy and sell quality pre-owned models such as Airstream Interstate – Atlas – Jayco Terrain – Tiffin GH1 – Winnebago Revel – Adventure Wagon – Pleasure Way Plateau – Ascent – Roadtrek – Leisure Travel Wonder – Unity - Coachmen Galleria – Thor Sanctuary and much more. Visit MHSRV.com for our complete line-up and the sale prices that have made us the #1 volume selling motor home dealership in the world 13 years in a row! About This RV This RV is powered by the RAM® ProMaster chassis, brought to life by a 3.6L V6 24V VVT 276HP engine and is approximately 17 feet, 10 inches in length. Inside, custom-molded ABS interior wall panels, a Uconnect® 5 NAV infotainment center and a swivel passengers seats bring high-end luxury to van life. The Arc includes many great features such as the Blind spot monitoring with rear cross path detection, LaneSense® lane departure warning with lane keep assist; Forward collision warning with active braking, rain-sensitive windshield wipers, wireless charging pads, drowsy driver detection, automatic high-beam highlights, heated holding tanks and much more. New for 2027 includes new sofa/bed system for easy set-up, new interior and moving to truma combi G system. About Us For more details on this unit, our full inventory, brochures, window stickers, videos, photos, and customer reviews, visit MHSRV.com or call 800-335-6054 for the Texas location or 334-288-0331 for our Alabama location. At Motor Home Specialist, we never charge prep or orientation fees. Every new RV sale includes a multi-point inspection, full interior and exterior detail. At our Texas location we also offer a high-pressure rain booth test—an exclusive service in the industry.
