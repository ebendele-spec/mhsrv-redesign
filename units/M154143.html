MSRP $1,210,426. New 2026 Newmar Essex 4551 for sale at Motor Home Specialist Features Bath & 1/2 Triple Slide Starlink Dishwasher Monitor on Passenger Side Security System Driver Seat with Massage Theater Seats 4 Merlin Solar Panels More About This RV Traveling with friends and family is easier than ever in this spacious coach that features a bath and a half. The full rear bath includes dual bath sinks, a Splendide stacked washer and dryer, plus a large 50' x 34' shower with a seat. There is even a large wardrobe with sliding doors here to keep all your clothes neat. The master bedroom includes a slide out king bed, plus a dresser and a TV. Your crew can get comfortable on the side hide-a-bed sofa or the jack knife sofa, and this area can become their sleeping space at night. There is also a Euro booth dinette that will be perfect for meal times or game night. This Essex kitchen is packed with luxury cooking amenities, like the Bosch 30' stainless steel convection microwave, the Bosch 21.6 cu. ft. stainless steel refrigerator, and the Bosch all electric, three burner flush mount induction cooktop. Your getaway resort starts with an Essex Class A diesel motorhome by Newmar. The Comfort Drive steering system puts you in control of your coach, plus there are air-cooled captain's seats for added comfort. Once you arrive at your destination, you can set up the dual Girard Nova side awnings that feature LED lights and wind sensors, and there are hydraulic leveling jacks for hassle-free set up. Each model features luxurious amenities, like the super polished solid surface countertops, the residential appliances in the kitchen, and luxury tile flooring. The Essex has you covered when it come to entertainment with Samsung LED TV's in the living area, bedroom, and overhead, plus there is a Bose 900 sound touch sound bar in the living room. You'll also appreciate the 12.5kW Cummins Onan diesel generator, the LED-Lit exterior storage compartments, three 15K heat pump central A/C units, and many more conveniences.
